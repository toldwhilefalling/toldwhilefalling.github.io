Told While Falling

Told While Falling is a short-form storytelling channel on YouTube and TikTok: revenge stories, workplace drama and plot twists, narrated over Minecraft parkour gameplay.

Told While Falling Uploader

The Told While Falling Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uploads and schedules the channel's own finished videos to the channel's own YouTube account through the official YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users.
